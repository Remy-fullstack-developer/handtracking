Train on your own hands

Like Teachable Machine, but on hand landmarks. Press Record, get ready for 1.5 s, then slowly move and rotate your hand while it captures samples. Samples are mirrored automatically so left and right hands both work.

  • For best accuracy record each gesture with different hands (ask friends, then Export/Import to merge) and different distances.
  • Record "No gesture" with a relaxed hand and random in-between poses so the model learns to stay quiet.
  • Record again in dim and bright light to make it robust.

Accuracy test

The app asks for each gesture in random order and records whether the recognised gesture matches, plus the time it took. Run it in different conditions and compare.
